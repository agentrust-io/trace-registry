## How to become a maintainer

**Maintainer**: 3+ merged substantive PRs and demonstrated judgment on the anchor
format or verification semantics. Nominated by a Maintainer, confirmed by the
Project Lead. Maintainers hold publish rights on the `trace-verify` package.

We would particularly welcome maintainers who **do not** work for OPAQUE Systems.
A registry whose only maintainers work for the operator that anchors into it is
asking to be taken on trust, which is the thing this repository exists to avoid.

## Running a mirror instead

You do not need to be a maintainer, or to ask anyone, to hold this operator
accountable. Running an independent mirror is the stronger check and requires no
permission from us: see [docs/mirroring.md](docs/mirroring.md) and
[MIRRORS.md](MIRRORS.md).
